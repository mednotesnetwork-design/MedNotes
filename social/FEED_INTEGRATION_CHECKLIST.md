# Feed integration gates

This branch is a development candidate only. Do not deploy or merge before review.

- Resolve follows, blocks and course membership on the server.
- Select only authorized published posts before building feed cards.
- Keep MCQ answers, flashcard backs, and signed media URLs out of cards.
- Test anonymous, follower, blocked, course-member and owner access.
- Validate stable pagination and mobile accessibility.
- Preserve provenance, correction history and private saves.
- Require successful build, typecheck and end-to-end authorization tests before release.
