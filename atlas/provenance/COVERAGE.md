# Release 0.4 coverage

476 retained knowledge records; 232 model objects. This restores 230 source-mapped objects previously excluded by the now-superseded asset restriction and retains 2 NIH clinical specimens. The 52 bone objects include attachment support copies and are not 52 distinct bones. Other objects: 62 muscles, 38 nerves, 28 arteries, 9 veins, 8 ligaments, 33 attachment patches. 183 records have direct assets. 48 landmark points are label locations, not fully segmented surfaces.

Search, independent layers, selection/isolation, bone study, muscle O/I/action/innervation, source attachment display and nerve/vessel topology are retained. Geometry remains source-mapped and awaits independent anatomical review. Missing segmented structures, sensory surfaces and validated animation rigs remain explicit. Production-ready is false; no claim of complete atlas-level validation.
